# Case #24: The Chronic Late-Paying Customer

![Cover Image](images/47.png)

### 📝 Technical Analysis
This case explores the **Opportunity Cost of Capital**. When a customer delays payment, the company’s **Operating Cash Cycle** is extended. This forces the firm to use "Working Capital" to fund the delay. If the firm uses a credit line to cover this gap, the interest expense directly reduces the "Net Profitability" of that specific customer. A customer who pays 90 days late is effectively receiving a 25% discount if the annual cost of capital is considered.

![Financial Proof](images/48.png)

### 💡 The Correct Action
1. **Late Payment Penalties:** Enforce strict contractual interest charges for payments beyond 30 days.
2. **Upfront Deposits:** Require a 50% deposit for all future orders from chronic late-payers.
3. **Cash-on-Delivery (COD):** Pivot high-risk, late-paying clients to a COD model until their account is cleared.
