# Case #29: Product Drift due to a Single Client

![Cover Image](images/57.png)

### 📝 Technical Analysis
This case illustrates **Strategic Misalignment** and **Market Opportunity Cost**. By over-customizing the product for a single large entity, the company has incurred high "Specific Development Costs" that do not translate into "Scalable Revenue." This is a failure in Product Management where the **Addressable Market** shrinks because the product becomes too niche. The long-term risk is that if the anchor client leaves, the remaining product is unsellable to the broader market.

![Financial Proof](images/58.png)

### 💡 The Correct Action
1. **Feature Generalization:** Only approve features that solve problems for at least 3-5 different clients in your target segment.
2. **Separate Service Wing:** If a client needs heavy customization, treat it as a separate professional services project with its own P&L.
3. **Strategic Roadmap Re-balancing:** Allocate a fixed percentage of development resources (e.g., 70%) to core market features that drive scalability.
