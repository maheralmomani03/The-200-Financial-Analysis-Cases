# Case #26: The Cost of Permanent Urgent Shipping

![Cover Image](images/51.png)

### 📝 Technical Analysis
This case examines **Logistics Cost Variance**. In many firms, "Shipping & Handling" is treated as a minor variable cost. However, when a client demands permanent urgency, the firm loses its ability to leverage "Freight Consolidation" and "Route Optimization." The technical focus here is on the **Fulfillment Cost per Order**. If the premium for expedited shipping is not passed on to the client, it acts as a direct reduction of the Gross Margin, often turning a high-volume client into a low-profit burden.

![Financial Proof](images/52.png)

### 💡 The Correct Action
1. **Dynamic Shipping Surcharges:** Implement a mandatory surcharge for any order requiring delivery in less than 48 hours.
2. **Batch Processing Incentives:** Offer small discounts to clients who consolidate their orders into weekly shipments instead of daily "emergencies."
3. **Cost-to-Serve Audit:** Perform a monthly review of logistics expenses per client to identify those who are "over-consuming" expedited resources.
