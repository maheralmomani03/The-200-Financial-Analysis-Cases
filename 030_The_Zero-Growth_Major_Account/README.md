# Case #30: The Zero-Growth Giant

![Cover Image](images/59.png)

### 📝 Technical Analysis
This case deals with **Opportunity Cost** and **Resource Allocation**. In a resource-constrained environment, every hour spent on a "Zero-Growth" account is an hour stolen from a "High-Growth" opportunity. The technical focus is on **Revenue per Employee** and **Growth Adjusted Margin**. While the giant provides stability, they also create "Capacity Bloat" without the benefit of scaling. Over time, the "Net Present Value (NPV)" of this relationship declines as inflation erodes the fixed-price margin.

![Financial Proof](images/60.png)

### 💡 The Correct Action
1. **Capacity Cap:** Limit the amount of total company resources any single non-growing client can consume.
2. **Inflation-Linked Pricing:** Ensure all long-term contracts with large clients include mandatory annual price escalators.
3. **Priority Re-alignment:** Shift your best talent to the high-growth segments and automate the servicing of the stagnant giant.
